## Summary

- AI Overview visibility,

- AI citations,

## Operational Takeaways

- Export historical GSC data before older baselines disappear
- Use branded vs. non-branded segmentation to evaluate content and brand performance separately
- AI-powered reporting workflows can accelerate SEO analysis
- Pages outside traditional top-ranking positions may still receive AI citations
- Monitor AI Overview exposure using external SEO visibility tools
- Refresh and optimize AI-cited pages regularly
- Conduct recurring quarterly SEO audits to adapt to evolving search behavior
- Re-evaluate search intent regularly as Google changes result composition
- Improve CTR performance for high-impression low-click pages
