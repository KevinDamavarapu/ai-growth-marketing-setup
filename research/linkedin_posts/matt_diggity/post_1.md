## Summary

Matt Diggity shares a structured SEO recovery framework used by The Search Initiative after one of their clients was negatively impacted by a Google Spam Update.

The recovery strategy focused on:
- algorithm recovery auditing,
- content quality improvements,
- backlink cleanup,
- commercial-page optimization,
- seasonal SEO timing,
- Google Merchant Center optimization,
- and topical authority building.

Over approximately five months, the strategy reportedly produced:
- a 96.54% year-over-year increase in organic sessions,
- and a 170.12% increase in organic revenue.

The workflow emphasizes:
- prioritizing commercially valuable pages,
- aligning SEO efforts with seasonal demand patterns,
- and building structured topical clusters instead of publishing isolated blog content.

---

## Key Insights

- SEO recovery requires active remediation rather than passive waiting after algorithm updates
- Commercial value should guide SEO prioritization decisions
- Thin content and over-optimized anchor text remain important spam-risk factors
- Seasonal timing significantly affects SEO growth potential
- Structured internal linking improves authority flow toward commercial pages
- Merchant Center optimization directly impacts Shopping visibility
- Topical clusters build stronger search authority than disconnected content publishing
- FAQ schema and structured content improve AI-search visibility potential

## Operational Takeaways

- Conduct detailed recovery audits immediately after algorithm impacts
- Prioritize pages based on commercial value rather than traffic alone
- Replace thin or over-optimized content with experience-driven information
- Build editorial authority using niche-relevant backlinks
- Publish and refresh content before seasonal demand peaks occur
- Optimize Merchant Center feeds alongside traditional SEO workflows
- Use structured topical clusters instead of isolated blog publishing
- Strengthen internal linking between informational and commercial pages
- Structured FAQ content may improve AI-search discoverability
