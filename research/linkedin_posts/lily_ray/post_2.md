## Summary

Lily Ray analyzes LinkedIn’s emerging SEO strategy involving curated “top content” pages built around high-performing LinkedIn posts for specific topics.

She observed that LinkedIn’s new `/top-content/` pages were already achieving strong Google visibility for queries related to topics like the Helpful Content Update.

The post explores how large authority platforms can leverage user-generated content (UGC) and programmatic SEO approaches at scale while potentially avoiding risks that smaller websites would face using similar tactics.

---

## Key Insights

- LinkedIn is expanding its SEO footprint using curated UGC-driven landing pages
- Programmatic SEO strategies can scale rapidly when supported by strong domain authority
- Large platforms may receive more algorithmic trust than smaller sites using similar approaches
- Search visibility increasingly depends on authority, trust, and ecosystem strength
- User-generated content remains a powerful discoverability asset when organized effectively

## Why This Matters

This post is highly relevant to AI-powered SEO research because it demonstrates how major platforms are evolving content discovery systems using:
- structured aggregation,
- curated UGC,

large trusted domains can often deploy aggressive programmatic strategies more safely than smaller publishers.

The insight reinforces the growing importance of:
