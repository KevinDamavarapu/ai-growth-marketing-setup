## Operational Takeaways

- Build centralized SEO intelligence systems using analytics and search data
- Include expertise and experience signals in AI-assisted content workflows
- Analyze both traditional search competitors and AI-cited competitors
- Extract important entities and structural patterns from top-performing pages
- Perform page-level topic-gap analysis before content creation
- Use differentiated positioning strategies instead of producing generic AI content
- Create detailed content briefs before generating content
- AI-generated content performs better when guided by operational SEO systems
- Semantic relevance and topical completeness increasingly affect visibility
