## Key Insights

- Freshness ranking depends on whether a query deserves newer information
- Historical authority remains important even for freshness-sensitive queries
- Query behavior and user demand influence freshness weighting
- Google evaluates multiple freshness layers including domain, URL, and document-version age
- Updated visible content can improve rankings while preserving historical authority
- Not all search queries benefit from fresh results
- Freshness systems interact with broader retrieval and ranking signals
- SEO updates should align with query intent rather than arbitrary content-refresh schedules

- Historical authority remains valuable within search ecosystems
