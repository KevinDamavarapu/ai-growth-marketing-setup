## Summary

Ross Hudgens discusses the strategic value of creating three-way comparison pages for highly competitive SaaS markets.

Instead of only producing traditional:
- “A vs B”

comparison pages, he argues that:
- “A vs B vs C”

pages can better reflect real buyer evaluation behavior in tightly clustered markets.

Examples include:
- Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz
- Salesforce vs HubSpot vs Pipedrive
- Vanta vs Drata vs Secureframe

Ross explains that these pages can:
- target unique search intent,
- support higher-funnel buyers,
- and create opportunities for smaller competitors to enter conversations dominated by larger brands.

---

## Key Insights

- Buyer search behavior is becoming increasingly multi-dimensional
- Three-way comparison pages can address search intent not captured by standard comparison content
- LLM-driven search experiences may encourage more conversational and complex comparison queries
- Transactional SEO content should increasingly align with nuanced buyer evaluation behavior
- Smaller competitors may gain more visibility opportunities in multi-brand comparison environments
