## Summary

Bernard Huang shares findings from an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) experiment using Clearscope’s Prompt Tracking system to analyze AI-generated web search fan-out behavior.

The experiment revealed that fan-out query frequency percentages are not simply indicators of popularity, but also indicators of query stability and consistency within AI-generated responses.

According to the findings:
- fan-out queries with frequency above 30% represent stable retrieval patterns,
- while queries below 15% reflect high response variability and inconsistent retrieval behavior.

Bernard argues that stable fan-out queries are more valuable targets for long-term content investment because AI systems repeatedly rely on them when constructing answers.

Low-frequency queries, by contrast, may represent unstable or experimental retrieval paths that could disappear in future AI responses.

---

## Key Insights

- AI search fan-out frequency can function as a stability signal, not just a popularity metric
- Stable retrieval patterns are more valuable for long-term AI visibility strategies
- LLM-generated search behavior remains highly variable for many prompts
- Low-frequency fan-out queries may indicate unstable or immature AI retrieval behavior
- AI search optimization should prioritize consistently retrieved queries over volatile ones
- AEO experimentation reveals operational insights that are difficult to observe without direct testing
