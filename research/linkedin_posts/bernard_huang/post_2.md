## Summary

Bernard Huang shares an update from an ongoing Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) experiment using Clearscope’s Prompt Tracking platform to test whether targeted content workflows can increase brand mentions inside AI-generated responses.

The experiment focused on prompts related to:
- Answer Engine Optimization,
- AI citation tracking,
- and AI visibility tooling.

After approximately three weeks, Clearscope’s visibility for one target prompt increased from:
- 0%
to
- more than 38% brand mention visibility.

Bernard attributes this improvement to:
- publishing content specifically aligned with the web search fan-out queries Gemini uses to construct answers.

However, the experiment also revealed that some prompts showed little movement — not only for Clearscope, but also for dominant brands like:
- Ahrefs,
- Semrush,
- and Surfer.

This led to a critical insight:
the issue was not content quality,
but prompt intent classification.

Gemini was interpreting certain prompts as:
- educational queries,
rather than:
- commercial recommendation queries.

As a result, the AI system returned:
- tactical educational content
instead of:
- brand recommendations.

The experiment was then adjusted by selecting a new prompt where:
- competitor visibility was already strong,
- but Clearscope had an initial foothold.

---

## Key Insights

- AI visibility optimization depends heavily on prompt intent classification
- Some prompts naturally trigger educational responses instead of commercial recommendations
- Strong AI visibility gains can occur when content aligns closely with AI retrieval behavior
- Prompt tracking can identify failed optimization paths early
- AI search optimization requires iterative experimentation and adaptive strategy changes
- Brand mention tracking provides a new operational KPI for AEO workflows
- Prompt selection can be more important than content quantity

## Why This Matters

This post demonstrates that:
AI visibility optimization is not simply about publishing more content.

Instead, success depends on understanding:
- how LLMs interpret prompts,
- how retrieval systems classify intent,
- and how AI systems decide between:
  - educational outputs,
  - tactical guidance,
  - and commercial recommendations.

The experiment also highlights the importance of:
- operational feedback loops,
- prompt validation,
- and iterative optimization
within Answer Engine Optimization workflows.

Rather than blindly scaling content production, Bernard emphasizes:
- testing,
- measurement,
- and strategic prompt selection.

---

## Operational Takeaways

- Validate prompt intent before investing heavily in content production
- Track brand mention visibility over time rather than relying only on traditional SEO metrics
- Identify whether prompts trigger:
  - educational,
  - informational,
  - or commercial AI responses
- Use iterative experimentation to refine AEO strategy
- Focus on prompts where competitors already demonstrate visibility potential
- Treat AI optimization as a dynamic testing process rather than a static ranking exercise
