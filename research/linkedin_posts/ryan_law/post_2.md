## Summary

Ryan Law discusses an internal analysis of the Ahrefs blog involving:
- 1,642 articles
- and more than 15 products.

The analysis revealed major visibility gaps between products and blog content.

Examples:
- 366 articles did not explicitly mention a single Ahrefs product
- Site Explorer appeared in 47% of posts
- newer products appeared in fewer than 6% of posts

Ryan explains how he built an AI-assisted analysis tool that:
- scraped blog content,
- identified product mentions,
- measured mention intensity,
- integrated SEO metrics,
- and generated prioritized content update recommendations.

The system helped identify opportunities to improve:
- product visibility,
- internal content alignment,
- and content-driven product exposure.

---

## Key Insights

- Large content libraries often contain significant product visibility gaps
- AI-assisted analysis can help scale editorial and product-content optimization
- Content marketing performance should be evaluated beyond traffic alone
- Product mention intensity and contextual relevance matter in SaaS content ecosystems
- AI tools can support scalable content auditing and prioritization workflows
