## Summary

Ryan Law discusses research investigating whether adding schema markup improves citations in AI search systems such as:
- Google AI Overviews,
- AI Mode,
- and ChatGPT.

While previous large-scale analysis of 6 million URLs showed that heavily cited pages often contain schema markup, Ryan explains that this relationship may reflect correlation rather than direct causation.

To test this, researchers analyzed 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD schema markup and compared AI citation visibility before and after implementation.

The result:
no measurable improvement in AI citations was observed.

---

## Key Insights

- Schema markup alone does not appear to directly increase AI citation visibility
- Many perceived AI SEO “hacks” may actually reflect correlation rather than causation
- Pages with strong SEO foundations are more likely to implement schema correctly
- AI search optimization requires broader content quality and visibility strategies beyond technical markup alone
- Evidence-based testing is increasingly important in emerging AI search ecosystems

---

## Why This Matters

This post is highly relevant to AI-powered SEO research because it highlights the importance of:
- experimental validation,
- evidence-based optimization,
- and avoiding simplistic assumptions in AI search strategy.

The findings suggest that:
technical SEO elements such as schema markup still matter,
but they are unlikely to function as standalone solutions for AI visibility.

Instead, successful AI discoverability strategies likely depend on broader factors such as:
- content quality,
- authority,
- trust,
- citations,
- and overall SEO strength.
