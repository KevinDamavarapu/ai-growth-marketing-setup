## Summary

Kevin Indig discusses how AI models treat different types of websites differently when generating answers. Based on Semrush data, strong consumer brands such as Google, Apple, and LinkedIn receive frequent direct mentions in AI-generated outputs, while publishers and aggregators are more commonly cited as sources without being explicitly named.

The post highlights the distinction between brand mentions and citations within AI search ecosystems.

---

## Key Insights

- AI systems appear to favor strong consumer brands for direct mentions
- Publishers and aggregators are more likely to function as citation sources rather than recognized entities
- Brand recognition may become increasingly important in AI-driven search environments
- Traditional authority signals may evolve differently in AI-generated search experiences

---

## Why This Matters

This insight is highly relevant to AI-powered SEO because it suggests that future optimization strategies may require more than ranking and citations alone. Building recognizable brand authority could become critical for visibility in AI-generated answers and conversational search systems.
