## Summary

Kevin Indig analyzed citation patterns across G2 profiles and found that paid profiles receive significantly more citations than free profiles. Across 1,339 G2 categories containing both paid and free listings, paid profiles outperformed free profiles in 94.2% of cases, with a median citation ratio of 12.6x.

The post argues that the difference is not driven solely by review count, but also by profile completeness and investment in profile optimization.

---

## Key Insights

- Structured third-party platforms influence discoverability and citations
- Profile completeness contributes to visibility in search ecosystems
- Brand authority can offset weaker profile optimization, but only for dominant companies
- AI-powered search and citation systems may reward structured and well-maintained data sources

---

## Why This Matters

This post is highly relevant to AI-powered SEO because it highlights how external authority platforms and structured profile optimization affect discoverability and citations. As AI search systems increasingly aggregate information from third-party sources, optimizing structured profiles may become a critical part of SEO strategy.
