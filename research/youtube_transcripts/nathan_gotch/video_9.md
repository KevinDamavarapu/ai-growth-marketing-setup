# Summary

In this video, Nathan Gotch argues that:
- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization),
- AEO (Answer Engine Optimization),
- and AI search visibility
are largely extensions of:
- traditional SEO,
- authority building,
- and broader digital visibility systems.

Nathan challenges the growing narrative that:
AI-search optimization is entirely separate from traditional SEO.

Instead, he presents a broader framework called:
# “Search Everywhere Optimization”

which expands SEO beyond:
- a company website
to include:
- AI citations,
- brand mentions,
- YouTube visibility,
- PR,
- influencer visibility,
- external authority signals,
- and retrieval-source presence.

The discussion uses a real-world brand example to demonstrate how:
a company with relatively weak traditional website SEO
can still dominate AI-search visibility through:
- off-site authority,
- branded mentions,
- PR coverage,
- and strong retrieval presence across external platforms.

The video repeatedly emphasizes that:
AI-search visibility increasingly depends on:
- total brand ecosystem dominance
rather than:
- isolated on-page optimization alone.
