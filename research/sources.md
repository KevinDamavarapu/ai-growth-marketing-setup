# AI-Powered SEO Content Production — Research Sources

## Research Objective

This research focuses on understanding how leading SEO and growth practitioners are using AI-assisted workflows for:
- SEO content production
- Programmatic SEO
- Content scaling
- Editorial oversight
- Workflow automation
- Human + AI collaboration

The goal is to identify repeatable systems and operational insights relevant to B2B SaaS growth.
