# LinkedIn Research Workflow

## Objective

Collect recent high-signal LinkedIn posts from SEO and growth experts discussing AI-powered SEO systems and search evolution.

---

## Collection Method

LinkedIn content was collected using:
- Manual review of recent posts
- AI-assisted categorization and annotation
- Structured markdown documentation

---

## Selection Criteria

Posts were selected based on:
- Data-backed insights
- Operational SEO strategies
- AI search relevance
- Content production workflows
- Search ecosystem analysis

Generic motivational or engagement-focused posts were excluded.

---

## Workflow Steps

1. Identify trusted SEO practitioners
2. Review recent LinkedIn activity
3. Select high-signal posts relevant to AI SEO
4. Extract core insights
5. Organize findings into categorized markdown files

---

## Observations

- High-quality SEO practitioners frequently share operational research rather than generic advice
- AI-assisted workflows still require careful human filtering to separate signal from noise
- Posts discussing AI search visibility and brand authority were especially relevant
