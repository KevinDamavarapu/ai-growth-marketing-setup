# References

## Overview

This document contains the primary sources used to develop the AI-Assisted SEO Content Production Playbook.

The recommendations throughout the playbook are based on research collected from ten leading SEO practitioners through LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, podcast interviews, and public educational content. Rather than reproducing any individual framework, the playbook synthesizes recurring themes, evaluates conflicting viewpoints, and documents the reasoning behind the final recommendations.

## Source Traceability

All original source URLs referenced during this research are preserved within the corresponding files in the `research/` directory. The repository paths listed throughout this document point to those files, where the original LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, podcast episodes, and other primary sources can be accessed for verification and further context.
