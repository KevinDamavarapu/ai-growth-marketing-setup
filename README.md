# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Research & Playbook

> A structured research repository and evidence-based SEO Growth Playbook built from insights shared by leading SEO practitioners specializing in AI-assisted content production.

---

## Objective

This repository documents research into AI-powered SEO content production workflows used by leading practitioners in B2B SaaS and content-driven businesses.

The objectives are to:

- Identify high-signal experts actively practicing AI-assisted SEO
- Analyze their content strategies, workflows, and operational insights
- Organize source material into a structured research repository
- Synthesize the research into an evidence-based SEO Growth Playbook and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The repository is divided into two complementary sections:

- **Research** – Collection and organization of insights from experienced SEO practitioners.
- **Playbook** – My analysis, recommendations, and decision-making framework developed from that research.

The goal is not simply to summarize expert opinions, but to evaluate them critically, identify trade-offs, and produce a practical workflow that can be followed by a marketing team.

## SEO Growth Playbook

The **playbook/** directory contains the final outcome of the research conducted in this repository.

Rather than summarizing individual experts, it synthesizes their recommendations into an evidence-based Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) supported by source citations.

The playbook also includes:

- Areas where experts disagree
- Recommendations intentionally excluded and why
- Original ideas developed during the research
- Limitations and assumptions
- Critical evaluation of expert applicability

---

## Research Methodology

The research followed a structured workflow:

1. Identify reputable SEO practitioners with proven experience in AI-assisted content production.
2. Collect primary-source material from LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, interviews, and public documentation.
3. Organize research into reusable knowledge assets.
4. Compare expert recommendations to identify recurring patterns and disagreements.
5. Synthesize findings into an evidence-based SEO Growth Playbook.

Whenever possible, recommendations are based on original content published by the experts rather than secondary summaries.

## Research Automation

To support scalable research workflows, the repository includes lightweight transcript automation tooling using Python and YouTube Transcript APIs.

### Implemented Workflow

- Automated YouTube transcript extraction
- Timestamped transcript collection
- Structured transcript storage
- AI-assisted transcript analysis workflows
- Manual fallback collection methods for unsupported videos

### Tooling Stack

- Python
- youtube-transcript-api
- GitHub
- Cursor IDE
- Claude Code
- AI-assisted content synthesis workflows

### Operational Workflow

1. Identify relevant expert interviews and podcast episodes.
2. Extract transcripts using API-assisted tooling.
3. Store raw transcripts for reference and traceability.
4. Generate structured summaries and insights.
5. Organize research into reusable knowledge systems.
6. Synthesize research into an actionable SEO Growth Playbook.

This workflow improves:

- Scalability of research collection
- Consistency of documentation
- Traceability back to original sources
- Efficiency when transforming expert knowledge into actionable playbooks
