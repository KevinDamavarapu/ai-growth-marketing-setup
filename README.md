# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Research

## Objective

This repository documents research into AI-powered SEO content production workflows used by leading practitioners in B2B SaaS and content-driven businesses.

The goal is to:
- Identify high-signal experts actively practicing AI-assisted SEO
- Analyze their content strategies, workflows, and operational insights
- Organize source material into a structured research repository
- Build a foundation for future SEO and growth playbooks

---

## Research Focus Areas

- AI-assisted content production
- Programmatic SEO
- Content scaling workflows
- Editorial oversight for AI-generated content
- SEO automation systems
- Topical authority building
- Human + AI collaborative writing workflows
